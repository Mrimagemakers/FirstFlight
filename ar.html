    • Android phones and tablets: open this page in Chrome and tap View in AR. Point at a table or the floor, then tap to place the model.

    • Meta Quest 3 / 3S / Pro: open in the Quest Browser and tap View in AR to see the model in passthrough. Trigger places it; the thumbstick rotates and resizes; A explodes, B shows labels.

    AR needs a secure (https) connection and a device that supports WebXR or AR Quick Look.
