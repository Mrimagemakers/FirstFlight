## Long description
First Flight puts you on the ramp, in the cabin, in the maintenance hangar and in the cockpit, so you can learn how aviation work really happens before your first day on the job.

**Five hands-on training stations**
- **Ramp & cargo safety.** Walk a working gate with a narrow-body airliner, belt loader, baggage carts and cargo containers. Find foreign object debris (FOD), turn on the engine intake and jet-blast hazard areas, and decide what to do when a package leaks or someone asks you to drive equipment you aren't qualified on.
- **Cabin safety & appearance.** Clear the cabin before boarding, find the fire extinguisher, the PED fire containment kit, oxygen and the exits, and handle real cabin situations such as an overheating phone or an unlabeled cleaning product.
- **Aircraft systems lab.** Reveal the engine, fuel and electrical systems of a training airplane, explode it into parts, watch the controls move, and complete a tool-control check like a maintenance technician.
- **Preflight walkaround.** Eight learning stations around a training airplane.
- **Fly.** Take the controls in a detailed cockpit with working instruments. Fly four missions over six realistic regions: Alpine Lakes, Pacific Coast, Red Rock Canyon, Emerald Islands, Northern Highlands and Blue Hour Valley.

**Built for VR and for every screen**
Play seated or standing in a Meta Quest headset with teleport, snap turning and smooth movement, a menu that rides on your hand and hand-tracking support. Floating lesson panels go wherever you need them. The same experience runs on desktop, laptop, tablet and phone with mouse, touch or a gamepad.

**Plan a real career**
The built-in Aviation Careers & New-Hire Hub covers 15 airport and airline roles, a 90-day new-hire plan, a Day-1 orientation agenda, orientation scenarios and practical tools such as an application tracker and a training passport. Its Aviation Maintenance Center adds 14 maintenance careers, a step-by-step FAA mechanic (A&P) pathway, 11 interactive tutorials on tool control, technical data, records, torque, safety wire, rivets, human factors, corrosion, NDT, inspections and hangar safety, and working technician tools. It links to official FAA, IATA, OSHA and employer resources.

First Flight is an educational simulation. It does not provide flight-training credit, certification, airport access or employment qualification.
