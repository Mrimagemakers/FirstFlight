Privacy policy

Effective October 17, 2026 · PenGame Publishing, LLC

First Flight is designed to work without collecting personal information.

What we don’t collect

  • No accounts, sign-in or profiles.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No camera, microphone or location access.

What stays on your device

Your training progress, settings and any records you enter in the careers tools (such as an application tracker or training passport) are saved in your browser’s local storage on your device. They are never sent to us. You can erase them at any time from Settings → Reset training progress, or by clearing this site’s data in your browser or headset.

VR and motion data

When you use a VR headset, head and controller positions are processed in real time on your device to draw the scene. They are not recorded, stored or transmitted.

Links to other sites

The careers hub links to official resources and employer career sites (for example FAA, IATA, OSHA, Delta and United). Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

First Flight is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
